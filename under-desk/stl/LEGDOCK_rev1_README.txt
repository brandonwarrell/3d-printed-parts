LEG-DOCK rev1 — vertical power-strip dock for the 95x95 back leg
=================================================================
Replaces the keyhole approach entirely. Two parts + one velcro strap.
Sized to YOUR strip: 42.5 x 25.2mm body -> pocket 45.0 x 27.7 (2.5 slack,
the strap makes it snug).

PRINT (both parts): ABS, 0.2mm, no supports, AS ORIENTED (flat plates on
bed), brim recommended. SHOE ~45g / ~2h, CLIP ~18g / ~50min.
Note: the shoe's lip roof prints as a short internal bridge — slight sag
inside is normal and harmless (clearance absorbs it).

INSTALL:
1. Screw the SHOE to the leg's flat face, cord-slot side facing OUT,
   about 250-300mm above the floor (2x #8 wood screws, countersunk).
   The pocket mouth faces UP.
2. Hold the strip above it, drop its cord end into the pocket; lay the
   cord sideways into the front slot as it goes in.
3. Screw the CLIP to the leg just below the strip's TOP end (same
   screws). Thread a velcro strap through the two side slots, around
   the strip, cinch.
4. Sockets face out into the room. Give the mains lead a relaxed loop
   before it runs to the wall outlet.

FIT REPORT (tell me which):
- drops in with easy slack, strap cinches solid   <- target
- won't seat / has to be forced                   -> pocket grows
- sloppy even strapped                            -> pocket shrinks
Also note if the cord slot fights the cord's molded strain relief.

The KEY_GAUGE print is retired — no longer needed. Same-filament rule:
these calibrate your ABS profile; later parts print with the same.
