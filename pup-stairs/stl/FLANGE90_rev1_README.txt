FLANGE-90 rev1 — corner glue-rivet bracket (structural, final)
==============================================================

WHAT IT DOES: sits in a box corner; hot glue pressed THROUGH the eight
6 mm holes forms mechanical rivets, turning a weak butt joint into a big
shear joint. Hot glue is strong in shear, weak in peel — this bracket is
what lets the whole hot-glue staircase work.

PRINT: ABS (final part, standing rule), as exported — long leg flat on the
bed, no supports. 45° gussets print clean; slight droop inside the four
horizontal holes is fine (they're glue ports). ~17 g, ~40-45 min.
Brim recommended (ABS + small footprint corners). Print 2 for the coupon
test first; the full build wants ~24.

COUPON-1 (do this before buying all the wood): hot-glue one flange between
two ply scraps at 90° — bead along the corner, then FILL the holes with
glue so it mushrooms through. Let cure 10 min. Try hard to break it.
  - breaks at the wood        -> joint strategy confirmed, build
  - glue pops off the plastic -> scuff pads with sandpaper, retest
  - plastic snaps             -> report where, I thicken that feature
